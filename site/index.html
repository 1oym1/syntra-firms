One fire record. Two satellite eras.

SYNTRA merges 26 years of NASA MODIS and VIIRS hotspots into one consistent record for Sudan, so a bad fire season looks bad because of fire, not because a sharper satellite arrived.

Raw MODIS predicts what VIIRS saw with R² 0.36. After SYNTRA's harmonization: 0.993, tested leave-one-year-out on 119 months.

Under the hood

From raw pixels to a record you can plan with

DOWNLOAD4,462,802

clean hotspots, 2000–2026, from the FIRMS archive and the near-real-time API.

CLEAN45,464

always-hot sites set aside: gas flares, industry and other static heat sources.

HARMONIZE0.1°

fire cell-days: each grid cell counts once a day, then MODIS is converted month by month.

VALIDATER² 0.993

leave-one-year-out on 119 months. NOAA-20 × 0.986 takes over where Suomi NPP data ends.

Does it hold up?

Tested on years it never saw

119 held-out months per country. It works where vegetation burns. In Saudi Arabia, with few real fires and many gas flares, it does not, and we show that too.
